# Landing page (index.html → src/App.jsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: small shop owners in Uzbekistan, Uzbek first, mostly on a phone in the evening after closing. Action: call the call centre, write on Telegram, or ask for a demo. Proof on hand: real desktop and mobile screenshots, real hardware and prices, the five partner integrations. No usage numbers until the owner sends them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one metro line through a shop's day. Every section is a stop on a single green line, and the visitor rides from the till to the tariff to a person on the phone. It refuses the category's stacked hero plus equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Tashkent Metro at night. Tunnel ink ground, marble-white type, the line in iDokon green, glazed cobalt tile halls for the two quiet moments. (Brass stop numbers were dropped at build: the craft floor bans decorative numbering.) Station signage type (Unbounded) for headlines and stop names, Onest for reading, till digits for money. Raise (from the declined azulejo hall): layouts recompose by whole tiles, and the cobalt halls admit their grout grid.

STORY: The visitor sees the real till in the first stop, rides past hardware, integrations, the phone app and tariffs, and arrives at the terminal stop, Aloqa, with a call button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The line starts at a large terminus roundel beside a left-aligned signage headline; subcopy and the demo and contact actions sit under it; the desktop till with the phone payment screen fills the lower half, crossing the fold.

FORM: grounded candidate 7 of 7 (Tashkent Metro), seed:f6ede43fb86de475. Signature interaction: a "you are here" train marker rides the rail at mid-viewport while stops fill as they pass (scroll-driven CSS); the nav is a line map whose current stop fills.
